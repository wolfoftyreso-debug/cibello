What to eat tonight? Dinner ideas from what you already have

“What should we eat tonight?” is rarely a lack of recipes. It is a lack of a starting point. Recipe sites give you thousands of options and none of them know what is in your fridge. Here is a better way to decide, and how Cibello does it for you.

The dinner picker: a suggestion in 10 seconds

The picker chooses from forty everyday dishes and needs no account. In the app, suggestions start instead from what is actually in your fridge.

Why the dinner question is so hard

Deciding what to cook means juggling four things at once: what you have, how much time there is, what everyone will eat and what should be used up before it spoils. Doing that in your head every evening is real mental load. Most people default to the same three dishes or to takeaway, not because they lack ideas but because deciding is exhausting.

Start with the fridge, not the recipe

Cibello flips the usual order. Photograph your fridge, freezer and pantry, or scan a receipt, and the app builds a food inventory. When you ask “what should we eat?” it matches that inventory against more than 9,000 recipes and ranks them by how much you already have at home, so a dish with 90% of the ingredients in your kitchen comes first. Each suggestion explains why: “because you have chicken and cream that expire soon, and you tend to like creamy dishes.”

Quick dinner ideas that almost always work

  • Pasta with whatever is in the fridge: cheese, vegetables, a protein, 15–20 minutes.
  • Omelette or frittata: rescues eggs, leftover vegetables and cheese.
  • Stir-fry with rice or noodles: half vegetables, any protein, soy sauce.
  • Tacos or wraps: mince, beans or chicken, everyone builds their own.
  • Soup from the pantry: lentils, canned tomatoes, root vegetables.
  • Sheet-pan dinner: frozen fish or chicken with potatoes and vegetables.

What these dishes share is flexibility. Cibello shows which ingredients you have and which are missing, so you can see at once whether a recipe works as it is or needs adapting.

Plan tonight, and the rest of the week

Once tonight is solved, the same food inventory powers the weekly meal planner, which varies dishes and ingredients and drops the missing items into a shared shopping list. Frequently asked questions

How do I decide what to eat tonight?

Start from what you have. Cibello builds a food inventory from photos or receipts and ranks recipes by how many ingredients are already at home, weighted by what should be used first and what your household likes.

Can Cibello suggest dinner from the ingredients I have?

Yes. The app matches your reviewed food inventory against more than 9,000 recipes and shows what may be missing.

Does it consider food that is about to expire?

Yes. Items nearing their date are prioritised in suggestions, so dinner also reduces food waste.

Does Cibello handle allergies?

You can set allergies and diets, but filters are guidance, not a guarantee. Always check ingredients and packaging yourself.
