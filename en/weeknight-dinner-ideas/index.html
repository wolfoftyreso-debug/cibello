Weeknight dinners, sorted by time

25 weeknight dinner ideas, sorted by how long they take

On a weeknight the question is rarely what would be nice but what can be done in the time there is. These 25 dinners are sorted by minutes, use ingredients most kitchens hold, and can be varied by swapping the protein. At the end: how Cibello makes sure the ingredients are actually there.

Ready in 15 minutes

These are for the evenings when the cooking has to be shorter than the commute. They lean on the cupboard and on whatever is left in the fridge, and none of them needs a recipe once you have made them twice.

  • Tuna pasta: pasta, tinned tuna, crème fraîche or a spoon of mayonnaise, lemon.
  • Egg fried rice: yesterday's rice, eggs, frozen vegetables, soy sauce.
  • Tortilla pizzas: tortillas, passata, cheese and whatever needs using.
  • Pesto pasta with chicken: leftover roast chicken or a ready-cooked breast.
  • Omelette with cheese and vegetables: rescues eggs and half-used vegetables.
  • Halloumi wraps: halloumi, tortillas, salad, yoghurt dressing.
  • Beans on toast, upgraded: good bread, beans, grated cheese, a fried egg on top.

Ready in 20 to 25 minutes

A little more time buys a hob and an oven working together, or a sauce that has had a few minutes to come together. Most of these feed four from a normal weekly shop.

  • Sausage stroganoff with rice.
  • Chicken stir-fry with noodles.
  • Tacos with mince, chicken or beans.
  • Spaghetti carbonara.
  • Baked salmon with potatoes and lemon.
  • Falafel in pitta with salad.
  • Tomato soup with grilled cheese sandwiches.
  • Chickpea and spinach curry.
  • Bubble and squeak from leftovers, with a fried egg.
  • Salmon poke bowl with rice and avocado.

Ready in 30 minutes

Half an hour is enough for a stew or a bake, and most of these double happily for lunchboxes later in the week. Cook one of them on a quieter evening and one hectic night later in the week is already solved.

  • Creamy chicken and mushroom casserole.
  • Lentil soup with bread.
  • Vegetable chilli.
  • Chicken curry with coconut milk: cook double for the freezer.
  • Meatballs with mash.
  • Sausage and root vegetable traybake.
  • Pasta bake with broccoli and cheese.
  • Bean burgers with potato wedges.

More quick dishes, with a table of times and ingredients, are in the guide to quick dinner ideas. With children at the table: family dinner ideas.

The trick: let the fridge choose

Every dish above assumes the ingredients are actually in the house. That is where weeknight dinner usually falls apart: you choose the dish first and then discover the peppers are gone. Cibello turns the order around. Photograph the fridge and cupboards, and the app knows what is there; recipes with ingredients you have are ranked by how much of each dish is already in your kitchen, with items close to their date at the top. Four weekdays at a time become a weekly meal plan, and whatever is missing lands in a shared shopping list. You review each scan before it is saved, because AI can misread a label.

Rotation beats inspiration

Most households eat 10 to 15 dishes on rotation, and there is nothing wrong with that. The trick for variety is to swap the protein and the sides rather than the whole dish: the same casserole with chicken one week and chickpeas the next, the same tacos with fish instead of mince. Cibello learns what you tend to choose and suggests variations that resemble your favourites but use what is in the kitchen right now. Read more about everyday cooking that lasts the whole week.

Make Monday easier on Sunday

Ten minutes on Sunday evening is enough to place three or four of the dishes above on the days when they fit, and to check that the ingredients for the 15-minute options are in the house. That is the whole system. If the week changes, the plan changes with it, and any dish that did not get cooked simply moves. Fewer decisions at six o'clock, less food forgotten, and the same rotation of dishes everyone already likes.

If Sunday evening is not realistic, any ten minutes will do: on the bus, in a waiting room, or while the kettle boils on Monday morning. The point is not the day but the habit of deciding once for several evenings instead of once every evening. After a few weeks the plan mostly writes itself, because the same dishes keep coming back in a slightly different order.

Frequently asked questions

What are good weeknight dinner ideas?

Dishes that take 15 to 30 minutes and use items most kitchens hold: pasta, omelettes, stir-fries, tacos, soups, casseroles and traybakes. The list above has 25 of them.

How do I find dinner ideas based on what I have at home?

Photograph your fridge and cupboards in Cibello. The app ranks more than 9,000 recipes by how much of each dish is already in your kitchen.

How do I get variety into weeknight meals?

Swap the protein and the sides rather than the whole dish, plan three to four dinners at a time and include a leftovers night.

Can Cibello plan the whole week?

Yes. The weekly meal plan varies dishes and ingredients and adds whatever is missing to a shared shopping list. You review and adjust the plan yourself.
