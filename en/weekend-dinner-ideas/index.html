Weekend dinner ideas: Friday night, Saturday supper and the Sunday pot

The weekend runs on different rules from the working week. There is more time, more often guests, and usually a bit more appetite for cooking something properly. Here are ideas for Friday, Saturday and Sunday, and a way of letting the weekend's cooking give the week ahead a head start.

Friday night without the fuss

Friday is the evening the week finally lets go, and hardly anyone wants to stand at the hob for an hour. Pick something everyone builds for themselves, or something that is on the table in twenty minutes. The plates are allowed to look a little untidy.

  • Tacos or fajitas: everyone fills their own, which suits children and guests alike.
  • Homemade pizza: make the dough in the morning or use a ready-made base, and top it with whatever is left in the fridge.
  • Burgers with oven-baked potato wedges, with or without meat.
  • Prawn linguine with lemon and garlic: twenty minutes from pan to plate, yet it feels like an occasion.
  • Bowl night: rice, salmon or tofu, avocado, pickled red onion and whatever else is around.
  • Pulled pork or pulled jackfruit in soft rolls with a sharp coleslaw.
  • Fish finger sandwiches done properly: good bread, crisp lettuce, a spoon of tartare sauce.

Saturday: simple but a bit special

A special dinner does not have to be a complicated one. Choose one ingredient to be the centre of the plate and keep everything around it plain: good bread, a green salad, a sauce you have made before.

  • Oven-baked salmon with dill, lemon and roast potatoes.
  • Roast chicken thighs with root vegetables and thyme, all on one tray.
  • Fish soup with saffron and aioli, with crusty bread for dipping.
  • Mushroom or asparagus risotto, depending on the season.
  • Steak with béarnaise and potato wedges.
  • Vegetarian lasagne with spinach and ricotta.
  • A grazing board or tapas made from what is already in: cheese, olives, roasted vegetables, a dip, some cured meat if you have it.

When people are coming round

Cook something for guests that you have made at least once before and that can be largely prepared in advance. That way you spend the evening at the table rather than at the cooker. Ask about allergies and preferences beforehand and check the ingredients yourself; the allergen filters in Cibello are guidance, not a guarantee. The time you save goes into a pudding or a proper side dish. A stew made the day before and simply reheated is, incidentally, a perfectly good dinner for guests, especially with a fresh salad and bread alongside.

Sunday: today's pot is Monday's lunch

Sunday is the day for dishes that are happy to sit on a low heat for hours. Conveniently, those are the dishes that are easiest to double.

  • Goulash or beef bourguignon.
  • Chicken curry in a big pot.
  • Chilli con or sin carne.
  • Lasagne or a pasta bake in two dishes instead of one.
  • A big batch of mince sauce: half becomes bolognese tonight, half goes into Friday's tacos.
  • Sunday roast with the lot, where the leftover meat and vegetables become a Monday hash or soup.

Cook double, cool the extra quickly and put it in boxes in the fridge or freezer. In Cibello those boxes can sit in your food inventory alongside everything else, so they are not forgotten at the back of the fridge and simply count as a meal on Monday or Tuesday.

Plan the weekend during the week

Weekend food often calls for one extra thing: a piece of fish, a particular cheese, a good loaf. If the weekend is already in your meal plan by Wednesday, whatever is missing lands on the shared shopping list and whatever you already have gets used first. That is one of the quieter ways the weekend helps you reduce food waste.

A weekend rhythm that runs itself

Many households find that a loose pattern takes the strain out of weekends: Friday something easy that everyone assembles, Saturday one dish cooked with care, Sunday a pot that also covers Monday. The pattern does not need to be strict. What it mainly does is remove the Friday afternoon question of what is for dinner, and make the Saturday shop fit what is already in the house. Run it for a few weekends and Monday tends to get calmer as well.

Frequently asked questions

What should we eat on a Friday night?

Tacos, homemade pizza, burgers, prawn linguine, bowls or pulled pork all work for children and guests and are on the table in thirty to forty minutes.

What is a simple but special dinner?

Let one ingredient take centre stage: oven-baked salmon, roast chicken thighs with root vegetables, risotto, fish soup or steak with béarnaise. Keep the sides plain.

How can weekend cooking help the week ahead?

Double a Sunday stew or curry, freeze the extra in boxes and put the weekend in your meal plan during the week so the shopping goes on one list.
