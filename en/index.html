Your kitchen, one clear overview

A meal planner and recipe app built around the food you have

Cibello connects your food inventory, recipes, weekly meal plan and shopping list. Scan your fridge or pantry, review what the AI found and get practical meal ideas from ingredients already at home.

Recipes with ingredients you have

Cibello can match your reviewed food inventory with more than 9,000 recipes and show what may be missing. Recipe and allergy filters are guidance, not a guarantee. Always check ingredients and packaging yourself.

Plan meals with less repetition

Build a weekly meal plan that considers your household, the food that should be used soon and better variety between meals. Every suggestion can be reviewed and changed.

Share the kitchen

Household members can work from the same food inventory, meal plan and shopping list, helping reduce duplicate purchases and forgotten food.

From scan to a useful overview

Use the camera for a fridge, pantry, receipt or barcode and then review the detected products before saving them. Correcting uncertain results keeps your food inventory useful and gives meal planning a clearer starting point.

Designed for everyday decisions

Cibello brings food storage, recipes, meal planning and shopping together so you can move from ‘what do we have?’ to a plan and a shared list. AI suggestions support the decision, while you remain in control of ingredients, allergies and what is finally saved.

A simple routine for the whole week

Start by reviewing what is in the fridge, freezer and pantry. Choose a recipe idea, place meals in the weekly plan and let the shopping list collect what is missing. When someone in the household buys or uses an item, the shared overview can be updated for everyone. This creates a practical loop from inventory to meal and back again, without requiring every product to be entered from scratch each day.
