# EU food waste facts (for the per-language statistics pages)

Source: Eurostat, "Food waste and food waste prevention – estimates" (Statistics Explained) and Eurostat news release 16 October 2025 "130 kg of food wasted per person annually in the EU". Reference year 2023. Cite Eurostat by name and link to
https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Food_waste_and_food_waste_prevention_-_estimates

- Total food waste in the EU, 2023: 58.2 million tonnes (fresh mass, edible and inedible parts). 2022: 57.8 million tonnes (+0.7 %).
- Per inhabitant, 2023: about 130 kg.
- Households: 53 % of the total, about 31 million tonnes, 69 kg per inhabitant.
- Manufacture of food products and beverages: 19 %, 24 kg per inhabitant.
- Restaurants and food services: 11 %, 14 kg per inhabitant.
- Primary production: 10 %, 12 kg per inhabitant.
- Retail and other distribution: 8 %, 10 kg per inhabitant.
- EU target (Waste Framework Directive revision, 2025): reduce food waste by 10 % in processing and manufacturing and by 30 % per capita in retail, restaurants, food services and households by 2030, compared with the 2021–2023 average. State this as the EU target; do not invent national targets.
